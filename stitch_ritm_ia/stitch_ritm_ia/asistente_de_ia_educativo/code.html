The Mezquita: A Forest of Stone

Imagine walking through a forest made of 856 columns of jasper, onyx, and marble. Here are 3 simple reasons why it's special:

  • 1 Double Arches: They used two levels of arches to make the ceiling higher and the building feel lighter.
  • 2 Color Pattern: The red and white bricks aren't just for looks; they make the arches strong!
